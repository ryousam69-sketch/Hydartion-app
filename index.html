    AI assistant

    Without a key, the assistant understands simple commands offline. With your own API key it understands any message. The key stays in this browser and goes only to the provider you pick. Use a key with a low spending limit, and never paste it into a shared file or chat.

    Keep reminders reliable

    Reminders are set with Android's alarm system, so they ring even when the app is closed. Some phones stop apps to save battery, so do these once:

    • Open Settings, then Apps, then Hydration, then Battery, and choose Unrestricted (or "No restrictions").
    • On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Infinix and Tecno phones, also turn on Autostart for Hydration.
    • If Android asks about "Alarms & reminders", allow it.
    • Open the app at least once a week. It schedules the next 7 days each time you open it.
    Why these times
    • A glass right after waking, then one about 30 minutes before lunch and dinner. Studies found 300–500 ml before a meal reduced appetite, mostly in older adults.
    • Smaller sips at your desk. The body absorbs about 0.8–1 L an hour, so no reminder is above 500 ml.
    • Big drinks stop about 2 hours before sleep. The bedtime sip stays small so it doesn't wake you.
    • No reminders while you commute. Missed water moves to the rest of the day.
    • Drink more on hot days, after workouts and when you're ill. Pale-yellow urine is a good guide. If you have a kidney or heart condition, ask your doctor about a 3 L target.
